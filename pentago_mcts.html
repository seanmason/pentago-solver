How to play

On your turn, place one ivory marble in an empty circle, then rotate any quadrant 90° using its ↶ or ↷ arrow. The first player with five in an unbroken horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line wins. A five-in-a-row made by placing a marble wins immediately, with no rotation required. If a rotation gives both players five, it is a draw.

“Take back” returns to your previous turn. The computer uses heuristic-guided Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS), not a solved-game database. Position analysis is a separate, approximate Monte Carlo model; it may disagree with the computer.

Independent browser implementation · Classic two-player Pentago rules · Move simulations and win percentages are approximate; even Expert may make mistakes.
