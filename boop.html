How to play and interpret the analysis

Each player starts with 8 kittens on a 6×6 quilt. Place a kitten on any empty square. It pushes every adjacent kitten one square outward, unless the destination is blocked. Pieces pushed over an edge return to their owner's supply. A cat pushes kittens and cats, but kittens cannot move cats.

Make three of your own pieces in a row, horizontally, vertically or diagonally, to remove that group and upgrade its kittens into adult cats. You choose which group to graduate if several qualify. Having all eight active pieces on the board lets you return one to your supply (upgrading a kitten if chosen). Win by making three adult cats in a row, or having all eight adult cats on the quilt.

The heatmap colors indicate approximate best follow-up outcomes, not which empty cell is guaranteed to win. Showing both perspectives is counterfactual: it evaluates what each side could do if it moved next. More playouts continues the existing trees rather than discarding earlier simulations. The win estimate uses the tree for the side actually to move.
